    What the result can and cannot support

    Frozen before final evaluation

    Three expanding, purged temporal development folds selected xgb_d3. Development included 30,888 appointments and ended on 25 May 2016. There was no final-test model reselection or recalibration.

    Uncertainty is substantial

    Against the rule, the precision difference was +5.61 percentage points (paired-date 95% interval +2.68 to +8.27). Against logistic it was −1.54 points (−3.41 to +0.43). Eight dates and two weeks cannot establish long-term stability; cluster-based intervals can be unstable.

    Raw probabilities overpredicted

    The primary model’s summed output was 4,453.7 expected no-shows versus 3,906 recorded. The observed/expected ratio was 0.877. ROC-AUC was 0.5996. No final-test calibration correction was applied.

    Unresolved transfer and allocation limits

    Appointment month was unseen for 71.21% of final appointments. Selection concentrated heavily in younger adults, with little allocation to older adults. These observations do not establish fairness, harm, or transportability to current clinics.

    This demonstration shows individual model computation only. It does not reproduce a roster allocation or offer a worklist. The historical 10% policy used descending full-precision scores, then SHA-256 of a fixed prefix and supplied appointment key, then the key itself to resolve ties. Patient or appointment keys are neither requested nor bundled here.

    A small, self-contained research tool with explicit boundaries. Local computation reduces transmission exposure. It does not make health-data processing automatically lawful or anonymous.

    IN THIS BROWSER

    A deliberately small footprint

    • The page downloads static code and one frozen model asset from the same host.
    • Model inputs and results stay in browser memory. They are not placed in request URLs, sent to a server, or written to browser storage by this app.
    • No analytics, third-party fonts, external scripts, embedded services, service workers, cookies, accounts, or background uploads are used by this app.
    • Editing removes stale results. Clear all and leaving the research view remove form values and result content. Reload and restored-page navigation reset the form.
    • No source rows, patient keys, example profiles, outcome labels, or private row-level predictions are bundled. No batch upload or export is offered.
    Important limits

    Clear all is not secure deletion from your device. Browser extensions, screen recording, operating-system memory, screenshots, accessibility services, or a shared device may expose what you enter. The app cannot control those systems.

    If this static page is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. Visiting links also contacts their destination. This is not a claim of total anonymity or zero host data collection.

    FROZEN RESEARCH CONTRACT

    Four inputs. Seven features.

    Required inputs are recorded integer age 18–110, valid source-calendar booking and appointment dates with at least three days’ lead, and the hospital/clinic neighborhood category (or an explicit missing value).

    The adapter derives lead days, log(1 + lead days), appointment and booking weekdays, and appointment month. It retains recorded age and the exact location category. Missing and unknown category encodings are distinct.

    The model contains 300 trees and uses 108 transformed float32 features. No outcome history, sex/gender, SMS timing, health-condition fields, or identifiers are inputs to this primary model.

    Printed source dates are a conservative research timing proxy. Historical snapshots, true time-zone semantics, outcome-finalization timing, cancellation/rescheduling rules, and identity linkage were not verified.

    Numerical equivalence and limits

    Offline checks used all 15,016 actual, target-free frozen source input rows. The browser adapter, feature transform and raw margins matched the frozen native implementation. In Node.js verification, four scores differed by at most 2.98 × 10⁻⁸ (two float32 units in the last place); historical daily 5%, 10% and 20% selections were unchanged.

    JavaScript math can differ at the last bit across engines. This is not a claim of universal bitwise probability equality or accuracy on all possible inputs. No new fitting, final-outcome evaluation, or synthetic input examples were used.
